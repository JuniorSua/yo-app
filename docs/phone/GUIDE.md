# Text Yo from your phone

Yo Phone is **your Yo in your pocket**. Text your Yo from your own phone, iPhone included, with no App Store and no
account. It's a web page at [yo-phone.vercel.app](https://yo-phone.vercel.app) that you add to your Home Screen,
where it works like a texting app. Yo does the work in a private workspace on your Mac or PC and texts you back.

A phone link is personal. Whoever has it talks to Yo **as you**. If a friend wants this too, they set up Yo Phone
for their own Yo.

## Make a link (in Yo, on your computer)

1. Open Yo and go to **Settings → Phone**.
2. Tap **Create phone link**.
3. Tap **Copy link** (or **Share**) and send it to your own phone, for example in a note to yourself or a text to
   your own number.

Or tap **Show QR code** and scan it with your iPhone Camera (or the Yo app). The code is the link, so tap **Hide**
when you're done. It also hides itself when you make a new link or leave Settings.

Or let Yo do it. Copy the ready-made prompt in Settings → Phone, *"Set up Yo Phone on my phone."*, and paste it into
Yo's chat. Yo asks for your OK with **Approve / Deny** first. When you approve, the link appears in
**Settings → Phone**. Yo itself never sees the link.

## On your phone: open it and add it to the Home Screen

1. Tap the link on your phone. It opens in Safari and connects.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open **Yo** from the Home Screen like any app.
4. If it asks for your link, paste it again. On iPhone, Home Screen apps keep their own storage.

Text Yo like a person. Under each message, **Sent** means it reached the relay, and **Delivered** means Yo has it.
When Yo needs a yes or no, a card with **Approve / Deny** shows up. Tap **Stop** to stop what Yo is doing. If you
send a lot at once, Yo may say it's busy. Wait a minute and send it again.

## Driving mode

Tap **Drive** at the top. A big microphone button appears.

1. Tap the microphone and say what you need.
2. When you stop talking, Yo sends it after 3 seconds. Tap to cancel.
3. Yo reads its replies out loud.

If your phone doesn't support voice typing in the browser, use the microphone key on the keyboard instead. Keep
your eyes on the road: set things up before you drive.

## Turn a link off

In Yo, go to **Settings → Phone** and tap **Turn off**, or tap **New link**. The old link stops working, and its
message history is wiped from the relay. If the relay can't be reached at that moment, Settings says so and Yo keeps
retrying. You can also ask Yo: *"Turn off Yo Phone."*

## Privacy

- **The link is your key. Keep it to yourself.** Anyone with it can talk to Yo as you, including your chats, files
  and approvals. Turn it off anytime.
- **End-to-end encrypted.** Messages are locked on your phone and unlocked only by your Yo, and Yo signs its replies
  so your phone knows they're real. The relay in the middle only holds scrambled text for a short time and can't
  read it.
- **The secret stays off the web.** The secret part of the link (after `#`) is never sent to the website.
- **Your computer stays closed.** Yo only reaches out to the relay. Nothing on your computer is opened to the
  internet.
