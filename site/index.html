Meet the team.

Yo leads and talks to you. Add a teammate whenever a job deserves its own: research, building, your inbox, writing, keeping watch. Each one has a name, its own memory and the model you pick. They message each other quietly, and a new one introduces itself and asks one question about its job.

  • YoLeadRuns the team and talks to you.
  • DewResearchReads, digs and cites its sources.
  • PicoBuilderCode, sites and fixes.
  • MochiInboxMail, calendar and follow-ups.
  • CloveWriterDrafts, edits and finds the tone.
  • KikiScoutBrowses, watches and tells you what changed.

Bring your own subscription.

Sign in with the Claude, ChatGPT or Grok plan you already pay for, and give each agent its own model. When a better one ships, you change a dropdown. Your agents keep their names, their memory and their work.

  • No API keys. No second bill. Agents use your plan and its limits.
  • Your password stays with them. You sign in on each provider's own page.
  • Mix them on one team. Claude on one agent, ChatGPT on another, in the same chat.

Its own computer. Not yours.

Your agents work on their own Linux computer, with a browser, a terminal and files. It runs on your Mac, or on a PC at home that stays on, and it never touches your own browser. Watch it work, or take over when a login needs you.

  • It asks first. You choose what needs your OK: purchases, messages, anything hard to undo.
  • Logins stay yours. You type passwords and codes yourself, or take over its screen.

Questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Yo is free and open source under Apache-2.0. There's no paywall, no premium tier and no Yo account. You pay your AI provider for your subscription, as you already do. If you'd like to, you can support Yo; everything stays the same either way.

Is using my Claude or ChatGPT subscription allowed?

Yo doesn't log in for you or hold your password. On Yo's computer you sign in with each provider's own tool and page (Claude Code, Codex or the Grok CLI), and your agents drive that tool, the way other open-source apps do. Each provider sets its own rules and limits, and they change, so check them: Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI. Agents can use up a plan's limits faster than chatting does. If a provider changes its rules, move that agent to another one. It's a dropdown, not a new app.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 13 or newer, with at least 16 GB of memory (32 GB is better) and about 20 GB of free disk space. And a Claude, ChatGPT or Grok subscription.

On a Windows or Linux PC, Yo runs in Docker and you use it in your browser: 8 GB of memory (16 GB is better), 4 cores and 15 GB free. You can also run its computer on an always-on home PC, so your agents keep working while your laptop is closed. How to install it on a PC

Is it private?

Yo runs on your machine. Your chats, memory and files stay there, and there's no Yo account and no analytics. Your requests go from your machine to the provider you picked, under your own account. Calendar, Mail and the rest of your Mac stay off until you turn each one on. Privacy policy

macOS says it can't verify Yo. How do I open it?

Yo's builds aren't notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm it once. Open Yo and click Done on the warning. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to “Yo was blocked” and click Open Anyway, then Open Anyway again and enter your Mac password. On macOS 13 or 14 you can instead right-click Yo in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. Or run this once in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Yo.app

Can my agents spend money or send things without me?

Only if you let them. You choose which actions need your OK, and they show up in Needs you. Yo doesn't solve captchas or know your passwords: for a login or a 2FA code, you type it yourself or take over its screen.

Who makes Yo?

An independent developer, in the open. Every release is built on GitHub's servers and published on its releases page. Found a bug? Use Report a problem in the app, or open an issue. More help is on the help page.
