08FAQ

Good questions.

How much does it cost?

Yo is $10, to support its development and updates. It's also open source, so you can build it yourself from source for free. Payments aren't open yet, so for now the app is a free download. Either way it runs on the Claude or ChatGPT plan you already pay for, so there's no model bill and no API key to manage.

macOS says it can't check Yo. How do I open it?

Yo's builds aren't notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm it once. Open Yo, close the warning, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway (macOS 15 and later). On macOS 14, right-click Yo in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. Or run this once in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Yo.app After that it opens like any other app.

After an update, macOS asks about “Yo Safe Storage”. Is that OK?

Yes. Yo keeps its saved login in your Mac's Keychain, under “Yo Safe Storage”. Each new version has a new signature, so macOS checks with you once after you update. Enter your Mac password and click Always Allow. Yo shows a short note while it waits.

Which subscriptions work?

Claude Pro or Max, connected with claude setup-token. Or ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business or Enterprise, through a Codex sign-in just for Yo. Each agent picks its account and model, and you can switch at any time.

Where does its computer run?

On this Mac, in a small virtual machine (Colima, free), or on an always-on PC at home if you're up for the advanced setup. Your first agent walks you through either one. A cloud option is coming soon.

Will it touch my browser or my files?

No. Each agent works on its own computer, with its own browser. Access to your Mac's apps, like Calendar or Notes, is off by default and you turn it on one app at a time.

What happens when it needs to log in or pay?

It stops and asks. Purchases, messages, and posts wait for an Approve tap. For logins and 2FA codes, you take over its screen, type them yourself, and hand control back.

How does it update?

Yo checks GitHub for new releases. When one is out, it tells you and a Download button opens the release page.

Does it keep working when my laptop is closed?

Yes, if its computer runs on a home server. Your Mac becomes a window into the agent wherever you are.

Yo, what's first?

Give it the thing you've been putting off. It'll handle the follow-through.
